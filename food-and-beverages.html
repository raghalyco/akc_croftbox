02 — Bakeries & Patisseries

We Build the Box Around Your Product, Not the Other Way Round.

Full-surface CMYK printing, soft-touch or gloss lamination, and gold or silver foil stamping on request. Your Logo, Your Box — Not a Generic Courier Carton.

Custom Compartment Counts Food-Safe Coating Festival Pre-Stocking Ribbon / Tie Closures

05 — Tea, Coffee, Spice & Packaged Foods

Cartons Engineered to Stand on a Shelf, Not Just Hold Product.

We make folding cartons and mono cartons for tea pouches, coffee bags, spice blends and dry snacks — with a moisture-resistant inner coating, reclosable tuck-ends or zip-lock windows, and a shelf-ready structural fold that stands upright on its own. Barcode and nutrition-label print zones are built into the die-line from the start, so your retail listing is compliance-ready on day one.
